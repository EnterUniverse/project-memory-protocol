# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-oriented collaboration environment. Participants should
discuss ideas and implementations rather than personal characteristics; distinguish observations
from interpretations; acknowledge uncertainty; and avoid publishing another person's private data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and
restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

Sensitive conduct or privacy concerns should be reported through the repository host's private
security-reporting channel rather than a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good faith,
minimize further disclosure, and document public outcomes only when doing so is safe.

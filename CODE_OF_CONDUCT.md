# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful and constructive. Discuss ideas and code,
assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and take care not to publish another person's private
information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.

Sensitive conduct or privacy concerns should be reported through GitHub's private security-reporting
channel rather than a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith while minimizing further
disclosure.
